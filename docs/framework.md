# Framework

Revenue Coach is designed around a simple constraint: an AI agent can produce far more plans, research, code, and artifacts than a person can validate in the market. Without explicit gates, that abundance becomes drift.

## The unit of progress

The primary unit is evidence moving through a revenue path:

```text
lead → conversation → diagnosis → proposal → paid commitment
     → delivery → result → repeat or referral
```

The earliest blocked stage is the current bottleneck. Later-stage optimization is usually deferred until the earlier stage has evidence.

## Three modes

### Cash now

For urgent income or an unproven paid path. The default is a narrow, manually delivered service or paid pilot using capability and access the user already has.

### Stabilize

For inconsistent acquisition, conversion, pricing, or delivery after initial paid evidence. The objective is repeatability with lightweight templates and procedures.

### Build

For compounding a proven channel, productizing repeated delivery, or creating leverage while protecting baseline cashflow.

Modes are sequencing tools, not identities. The user may move backward temporarily when pipeline or runway changes.

## Evidence hierarchy

Evidence becomes stronger as it approaches exchange and outcomes:

1. internal idea;
2. observed customer language or behavior;
3. relevant reply;
4. qualified conversation;
5. requested proposal or explicit buying signal;
6. paid commitment or deposit;
7. delivered result;
8. repeat work or referral.

The framework never calls early evidence certainty. It chooses the cheapest next test.

## WIP and build gates

The user maintains one primary revenue experiment and at most one long-term asset. New work must either move the current bottleneck or replace the active experiment through an explicit decision.

Software and automation require observed demand or repeated manual friction, a smaller alternative analysis, direct relevance to sales or delivery, and user approval. Coding ability is not treated as evidence that code is the right business intervention.

## Coaching loop

1. Ground in current reality and authorized state.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, fears, and missing information.
3. Locate the earliest revenue bottleneck.
4. Choose Cash now, Stabilize, or Build.
5. Recommend one move with explicit tradeoffs.
6. Execute or draft the smallest enabling artifact.
7. End with a 15–45 minute action contract and review time.

## Scoreboard

Core metrics are attempts, replies, qualified conversations, offers, paid commitments, cash received, delivered results, repeat work, and referrals. Time and cost are included when needed to judge economics or capacity. Content output, pages, code, and follower counts are supporting metrics only.

## Safety and autonomy

The agent may draft and analyze. External messages, publication, purchases, terms, pricing commitments, and account actions require explicit user confirmation. The coach does not guarantee income, fabricate evidence, or replace regulated professional advice.
