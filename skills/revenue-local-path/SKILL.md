# Local Revenue Path

Convert a selected local opportunity into safe, verifiable next steps. Do not assume national availability means the service operates or is recruiting in the user's city.

## Verify the path

Confirm, preferably from current official sources:

- service or employer availability in the city or practical travel radius;
- whether registration or hiring is currently open;
- eligibility, documents, vehicle or equipment rules, and screening;
- official app, office, registration channel, fees, deposits, training, and expected timeline;
- payout mechanics, commissions, operating costs, and major platform rules;
- safety, insurance, legal, tax, and account risks that materially affect entry.

Use local listings and recent worker reports to understand experience and demand, but distinguish them from official eligibility rules. Date the research.

## Estimate practical economics

Create a conservative range or break-even model from explicit assumptions:

- paid hours versus waiting or travel time;
- gross pay or order value;
- platform fee or commission;
- fuel, maintenance, data, parking, supplies, and financing costs;
- payment delay and cash needed before first payout.

Do not present anecdotal gross earnings as expected net income. Show which assumptions the user should test in one shift, conversation, or application.

## Define the support split

Physical work, identity verification, legal attestations, interviews, and final submissions remain user-owned. Depending on the collaboration contract, the agent may:

- research and compare official options;
- prepare a document checklist and registration walkthrough;
- draft a CV, profile, service menu, pricing sheet, or interview practice;
- find suitable apps or tools and explain setup;
- model costs and routes;
- prepare scripts for calls, visits, or customer acquisition;
- review evidence after the first attempt.

Never upload identity documents, accept terms, submit an application, or create an account without exact authorization and a supported secure tool. Do not store identity documents in coaching state.

## Output

Provide:

1. Verified availability and eligibility.
2. Official links or locations when research is available.
3. Required documents, tools, costs, and warnings.
4. Step-by-step registration or launch path.
5. Conservative unit economics and unknowns.
6. AI support plan and user-owned physical steps.
7. First checkpoint and fallback if registration, hiring, or demand is unavailable.

End with one safe, reversible next action such as verifying recruitment, preparing one document set, calling an official office, or testing one local customer conversation.
