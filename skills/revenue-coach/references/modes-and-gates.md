# Modes and Decision Gates

Use modes to choose sequence, not to label the user permanently. Reassess after meaningful evidence or a material change in runway.

## Mode 1: Cash now

Use when income is urgent, runway is short, or no reliable acquisition path exists.

Primary objective: create paid commitments quickly through a narrow, manually deliverable offer.

Default actions:

- contact warm leads and relevant communities;
- sell a diagnostic, implementation, repair, research, writing, design, or advisory service;
- use existing proof and capability;
- ask for a deposit or small paid pilot;
- deliver manually and capture language, objections, and outcomes.

Avoid: audience-building as the only channel, complex products, new apps, broad courses, elaborate branding, and unpaid custom work without a deliberate learning reason.

Exit evidence: at least one paid commitment plus a plausible next set of prospects, or clear rejection evidence that justifies changing the offer.

## Mode 2: Stabilize

Use when some revenue evidence exists but lead flow, conversion, pricing, or delivery is inconsistent.

Primary objective: repeat a working path without relying on heroic effort.

Default actions:

- tighten qualification and offer scope;
- standardize a lightweight proposal and delivery checklist;
- build a small follow-up cadence;
- improve proof with permission;
- raise or restructure price when evidence supports it;
- ask for referrals and repeat work.

Automation gate: automate only a repeated bottleneck that has occurred at least three times and whose manual process is understood.

Exit evidence: a repeatable acquisition source, understood conversion pattern, reliable delivery, and enough margin or capacity to invest.

## Mode 3: Build

Use when the user has sufficient stability to compound a proven channel, productize delivery, or create an asset.

Primary objective: increase leverage without breaking the revenue engine.

Default actions:

- productize a repeatedly sold service;
- deepen a proven distribution channel;
- create reusable intellectual property from delivered work;
- delegate a stable procedure;
- build software only from observed customer and workflow evidence.

Protect cashflow: keep a defined minimum of selling and delivery activity while building. If pipeline falls below that floor, return temporarily to Cash now or Stabilize.

## Reversible-decision rule

For low-cost reversible decisions, choose within one session and test. Reserve deeper research for decisions that are expensive, hard to reverse, regulated, or capable of materially harming the user or others.

## Stop conditions

Pause or change an experiment when its agreed test window ends and:

- the target actions were completed but no meaningful signal appeared;
- the market repeatedly rejects the same core premise;
- delivery economics are unacceptable;
- the tactic violates the user's values or constraints;
- a stronger opportunity is supported by better evidence, not novelty alone.
