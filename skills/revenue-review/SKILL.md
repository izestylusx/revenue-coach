# Weekly Revenue Review

Review decisions and evidence, not personal worth.

## Gather only useful evidence

If authorized state exists, read the current state, this week's log entries, and relevant pipeline rows. Otherwise ask for a concise summary.

Track:

- market-facing attempts;
- replies and qualified conversations;
- offers or proposals made;
- paid commitments and cash received;
- delivery outcomes, client feedback, repeat work, and referrals;
- time or cost only when it informs economics or capacity.

Vanity metrics may be noted but must not substitute for pipeline evidence.

## Review sequence

1. Commitments: what was planned and what actually happened?
2. Evidence: what changed in the market, pipeline, cash, or delivery?
3. Causality: what likely contributed, and what remains unknown?
4. Friction: where did the system or behavior break?
5. Economics: does the path appear viable at the current scope and price?
6. Focus: does the primary experiment continue, change, or stop?
7. Collaboration: did the work split, tool path, and checkpoints help or obstruct delivery?

Do not punish missed actions. Distinguish an invalid hypothesis from an unexecuted test.

## Decision rules

- Continue when useful signal appears and the next test reduces uncertainty.
- Change one variable when evidence points to a specific bottleneck.
- Stop when the test was executed, stop conditions were met, and no stronger learning justifies extension.
- Scale only after repeat evidence; do not add channels, software, or fixed cost because one week felt promising.

## Output

Provide:

- weekly scorecard;
- three strongest facts;
- assumptions updated;
- what to continue, change, and stop;
- the next week's single bottleneck and evidence target;
- one Monday or next-session action taking 15–45 minutes;
- any state updates, only if persistence is authorized.
- any capability, ownership, assistance-mode, or tool-routing change justified by observed work.
